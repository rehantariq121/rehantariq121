## Featured project

### ▸ [BIDX Coin](https://github.com/rehantariq121/BIDX-COIN-WEBSITE) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [Live ↗](https://bidx-coin-website.vercel.app)

A scroll-driven cinematic site for a proposed BNB Smart Chain eco-finance concept that
connects on-chain participation with traceable tree planting.

The page is built as one continuous film — a hero reveal, then six scroll-scrubbed video
chapters whose timelines are bound to scroll position rather than played. It includes an
interactive eco-ledger that lets a visitor trace a contribution from transaction through
allocation and field evidence to its 3/6/12/24-month survival checks.

Getting it production-ready meant rebuilding the media pipeline: **initial page load went
from ~101 MB of eagerly-fetched video down to 3.6 MB** through WebP conversion, dense-GOP
720p re-encoding for smooth scrubbing, and demand-driven chapter loading.

`React` `TypeScript` `Vite` `Motion` `ffmpeg`
